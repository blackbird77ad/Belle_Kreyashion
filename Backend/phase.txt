phase 4: Best Model
There are really 3 different money models here:

Belle as the affiliate
You recommend other brands’ products or tools and earn a commission when someone buys through your link.

Belle runs its own affiliate program
Other people promote Belle products, services, or digital courses, and you pay them a cut while still keeping the main sale revenue.

Belle as a marketplace/partner seller
You already look partly set up for this because the site has partner-product logic. In that model, vendors list through Belle and you earn listing fees, featured-placement fees, or commission per sale.

Best answer:
The strongest model is not choosing only one. It is a hybrid with Belle-owned sales at the center, partner sellers as the expansion layer, and affiliate offers only where they fill a gap.

Why this is the best fit:
Belle as only an affiliate is the weakest long-term model because it sends customers away, gives you the smallest margin, and makes the brand depend on other businesses.

Belle running its own products and services should stay the foundation because that gives the highest control over pricing, customer experience, repeat sales, and brand value.

The marketplace or partner-seller layer is probably the smartest growth move because your current setup already leans in that direction. It lets Belle carry more products, more price points, and more variety without having to own all inventory directly.

Then affiliate links can be used selectively for products or tools Belle does not want to stock, especially educational tools, beauty tools, digital services, or outside-brand add-ons.

Recommended structure:
1. Core revenue = Belle-owned products, services, and digital offers
2. Growth revenue = partner products listed through Belle
3. Extra low-risk revenue = affiliate links for complementary offers
4. Community growth = later add a Belle affiliate program so creators and loyal customers can promote Belle

Best practical path right now:
Phase 1: Keep building Belle as the main storefront and brand
Phase 2: Expand the partner-product model with clear commission rules
Phase 3: Add featured vendor placements and premium listing options
Phase 4: Add a Belle affiliate/referral program for influencers, stylists, and repeat buyers
Phase 5: Use external affiliate links only for things Belle does not directly sell

Simple conclusion:
The best model is Belle as a brand-led storefront first, a curated marketplace second, and an affiliate business third. That keeps control, increases margin, and still gives room to scale without carrying every product yourself.
